How to enable official LinkedIn OAuth
  1. Go to linkedin.com/developers/apps and select Create app. You need a LinkedIn Page for the company or project.
  2. Open the app’s Products tab and request Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect.
  3. On the Auth tab, add this Authorized redirect URL: http://127.0.0.1:8000/auth/linkedin/callback
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret from the Auth tab.
  5. Set them as environment variables, then restart app.py:
    LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID and LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET
  6. Select Sign in with LinkedIn above. Keep the secret out of the page and out of GitHub.
Official LinkedIn login confirms who you are and shares your name and email. It does not allow searching posts or reading recruiter emails. To find recruiters, use Open LinkedIn search below and paste the posts you copy.
